# Kalampaka + Meteora — visual reference sheet for a stylized diorama

Method: public pages only (Wikipedia EN/EL, Great Meteoron's own site, visitmeteora, sacred-destinations, allovergreece, IUGS geoheritage, Natura/IUCN summaries), read-only OpenStreetMap queries for positions and footprints, and about 25 Wikimedia Commons photos viewed in the browser. Nothing was downloaded, signed into or written to the project.

Evidence tags used below:
- **[S]** stated in a text source
- **[P]** my own observation from Commons photos (dated 2006–2024)
- **[OSM]** OpenStreetMap geometry or tags (approximate)
- **[?]** uncertain; see section 6

All hex values are eyeballed from photos, not pixel-sampled.

## 1. The six monasteries

Common vocabulary [P]:
- Walls: rubble or coursed local stone in warm grey-beige; some wings plastered cream. Katholika have ochre ashlar with red brick banding.
- Roofs: low-pitch hipped or gabled, orange-terracotta tile; domes are tile-capped drums with scalloped eaves.
- Dark-brown timber galleries and overhanging balconies on upper floors.
- Stone retaining walls rise flush from the cliff edge; cypresses and small gardens inside.

### Great Meteoron (Metamorphosis)
- **Siting:** on Platys Lithos ("Broad Rock"), the largest summit; buildings spread along one long edge, rest is courtyards and garden [P][S]. The hillside behind (NW) is higher than the monastery and carries the road and coach parking [P].
- **Elevation:** 613–615 m a.s.l., ~415 m above the valley floor [S en.wiki]; el.wiki says 534 m [?].
- **Footprint:** OSM precinct ~270 × 90 m (~1.8 ha) [OSM]; one Greek source says 50 stremmata [?].
- **Massing [P]:** long 3–4 storey guest/cell wing at the cliff edge with stacked arcaded loggias and timber galleries; a slim pale square tower mid-complex; katholikon group at the other end; stepped retaining walls below.
- **Katholikon [S]:** 1544/45 nave added to a 1387/88 sanctuary; four-column cross-in-square, Athonite type with side choirs and a large columned lite. 12-sided main dome on a drum, 24 m high (single source [?]). From the south, two tall polygonal tiled drums are visible [P].
- **Other buildings [S, monastery site]:** refectory 1557 (long, apsed, vaulted on five columns); hestia kitchen N of it (hemispherical vault with small smoke-hole dome); two-storey old infirmary 1572 (now museum); cave hermitage of St Athanasios left of the entrance; ossuary; cellar; chapel of Sts Constantine & Helen (1789).
- **Access:** rock-cut stairs with tunnel sections, cut 1923; count given as 146 by most sources, ~300 by visitmeteora [?]. Vrizoni net tower near the entrance [S]. A service cable car crosses from the car-park side (Commons category exists) [S/P].

### Varlaam (All Saints)
- **Siting:** whole summit of its pillar; rock ~373 m above the valley, 595 m a.s.l. [S].
- **Footprint:** OSM precinct ~80 × 60 m (~0.36 ha) [OSM].
- **Massing [P]:** buildings ring a paved courtyard terrace; high stone retaining wall with blind arches; 2–3 storey wings.
- **Katholikon [S]:** 1541/42, Athonite cross-in-square, two domes (naos and narthex); warm red-ochre masonry [P].
- **Distinctive:**
  - 1536 tower with windlass and rope net [S].
  - Small round/octagonal open kiosk with a conical red-tile roof at the tip of the terrace [P].
  - Switchback stone staircase with parapets on the cliff face down to a footbridge over the gap to the car park [P]; 195 steps cut 1923 [S].
  - Service cable car [S]; 12-ton oak water barrel; garden; chapel of the Three Hierarchs (single-aisle, timber roof); kitchen with octagonal dome/chimney [S].
- Great Meteoron is visible on the higher rock directly behind, to the west [P].

### Roussanou (St Barbara, nunnery)
- **Siting:** covers the entire top of a narrow vertical pillar; walls continue the cliff line so rock and building read as one mass [S][P]. Lower than its neighbours, 484 m a.s.l. [S].
- **Footprint:** OSM polygon 121 × 92 m includes the lower grounds; the summit building is far smaller, my guess ~30 × 20 m [?].
- **Massing:** three storeys; katholikon and cells on the ground floor, guest rooms and cells above [S]. Cream-plastered upper storeys over stone, red tile roofs, timber overhanging balconies [P].
- **Katholikon [S]:** small, built 1530 (or 1545), cruciform two-column Athonite type; one tall polygonal (octagonal) dome with one single-light window per face; sanctuary faces north because of the rock.
- **Access:** concrete stairs plus two small solid bridges of 1930 replacing an 1868 wooden one [S]; ~140 steps [S, approximate].
- **Base:** cypresses, ivy and gardens around the foot of the pillar [P].

### St Nicholas Anapafsas
- **Siting:** stacked vertically on a small narrow rock about 80 m high [S].
- **Footprint:** OSM precinct ~41 × 27 m [OSM].
- **Massing:** three to four stacked levels [S][P]:
  - entrance level: chapel of St Anthony and crypt
  - middle: katholikon with a low windowless dome (frescoes 1527)
  - top: old refectory/reception room, ossuary, chapel of St John
- No courtyard [S].
- **Look [P]:** sheer stone walls rising from the rock, top storey with dark timber balconies, orange tile roofs; a tiny detached belfry kiosk on the highest rock knob beside the building.
- **Setting [P]:** surrounded by dark fluted pillars; a ruined wall stump crowns an adjacent pillar.
- **Access:** footpath and steps up from the Kastraki road.
- **Status:** en.wiki's infobox calls it "extant but inactive as of December 2025" [?].

### Holy Trinity (Agia Triada)
- **Siting:** on a slender isolated pinnacle, over 400 m high, ~570 m a.s.l. [S]. Buildings sit on one part of the summit; the rest is garden and bare rock.
- **Footprint:** OSM precinct ~150 × 65 m (~0.8 ha); Wikipedia gives a summit garden of ~0.8 ha [S][OSM].
- **Massing [P]:** low 2-storey range with an arcaded ground-floor loggia, orange tile roofs, cypresses; low wall around a bare rock platform at the tip.
- **Katholikon [S]:** 1475/76, small two-column cross-in-square with one dome; brick-and-tile exterior; barrel-vaulted narthex 1689 (one source counts a second dome [?]). Rock-cut round chapel of St John the Baptist (1682) by the entrance passage.
- **Access:** path down from the road into the ravine, then ~140–145 rock-cut steps (1925) partly in tunnels/ledges on the cliff [S][P]. Service cable car/funicular from the road side; old winch in the courtyard [S].
- Kalampaka lies directly below; a footpath links the two [S]. Featured in the 1981 Bond film.

### St Stephen (Agios Stefanos, nunnery)
- **Siting:** whole top of a broad rock at the SE end, directly above Kalampaka; the monastery most visible from town [S]. About 575 m a.s.l. (510 m also quoted) [?].
- **Footprint:** OSM precinct ~80 × 70 m (~0.4 ha) [OSM].
- **Massing [P]:** fortress-like; long 2–3 storey stone ranges along the cliff edge with small regular windows, tile roofs, many chimneys; inner courtyard and flower gardens; small pavilion on a terrace at the cliff edge.
- **Churches [S]:**
  - old St Stephen (15th c., renovated 1545): small single-aisle church
  - new St Charalambos (1798): Athonite cross-in-square, multi-domed
- New church look [P]: cylindrical drums painted terracotta-pink with white arched pilaster strips and scalloped tile eaves, over ochre ashlar walls.
- **Access:** no steps; a short fixed footbridge from the road over a narrow chasm [S]. Bridge length not found [?].
- Bombed in WWII, frescoes damaged in the Civil War [S].

## 2. The rocks

**Geology [S]**
- About 95% pebbly sandstone and conglomerate (Oligocene–Miocene molasse, Gilbert-type delta deposits); the rest coarse sandstone with thin siltstone layers.
- Clasts: limestone, marble, gneiss, quartzite, ophiolite.
- Pillars up to ~200 m high and ~300 m wide [S IUGS, el.wiki]; cliffs "over 400 m" above the Pineios [S en.wiki].
- Vertical fractures plus water erosion produced the towers.

**Surface character [P]**
- Smooth, rounded, dome-topped forms; almost no sharp edges.
- Strong horizontal bedding ledges every few metres, studded with rows of pockets and shallow caves (tafoni) — especially on the wall above Kalampaka.
- Vertical fluting and dark water-stain streaks running from the rims down sheer faces.
- Pebbly "concrete" texture up close.
- Tops: mostly bare rounded rock with thin grass, moss and lichen, plus scattered low shrubs; only the broad summits (Great Meteoron, Holy Trinity, St Stephen) carry gardens and trees.
- Shrubs and ivy grow out of cracks and ledges; forest packs every gully between pillars.

## 3. Kalampaka and Kastraki

### Kalampaka (pop. ~8,400; area ~1.65 km²) [S]
- **History that explains the look:** burned by German troops on 18 Oct 1943 [S], so the fabric is almost entirely post-war.
- **Buildings [P]:**
  - Centre: 3–4 storey concrete apartment/hotel blocks; white or cream render; continuous balconies with black wrought-iron railings; awnings; shops at street level; some set-back top floors with terraces.
  - Elsewhere: 2-storey detached houses with gardens.
- **Roofs [P, my estimate from a 2015 aerial from Holy Trinity]:** roughly 85–90% terracotta-tiled pitched (hipped or gabled); 10–15% flat concrete terraces, mostly central blocks. Tile reads `#C2623A`–`#A9553A` [?].
- **Walls [P]:** white `#F2EEE4`, cream `#E8DFC8`, occasional ochre or pastel.
- **Streets [P][OSM]:**
  - Main axis is EO6 (E92), named Trikalon in town and continuing as Ioanninon; wide asphalt.
  - Side streets on a loose grid; pavements in grey/red concrete block pavers; black twin-lantern lamp posts; plane, mulberry and pine trees.
  - Rock walls close every northward street view.
- **Old quarter:** NE upper edge around the Koimisi church, with the Sopotos neighbourhood and its spring [S]; narrow lanes, stone retaining walls, courtyards with potted plants, cypresses [P][S].

**Squares [OSM]**
- Plateia Dimarcheiou: ~47 × 31 m at 39.7070, 21.6235. In 2014 it was a road junction with a round clipped-hedge island and a black twin lamp at its centre, plus a large pine [P]. Redesigned since [S] [?].
- Plateia Riga Feraiou: elongated ~62 × 23 m at 39.7057, 21.6259, on the main street; OSM maps two memorials there (Vlachavas, Chatzipetros).
- Old municipal market / Dr Liapis square: ~64 × 42 m, N of the town hall, partly parking.
- Plateia Dimoula: has the main playground [S]; location not found [?].
- Fountain: no source or photo confirmed one [?].

**Landmarks to model**
- **Koimisi Theotokou (Byzantine church)** — 39.7100, 21.6281; OSM footprint ~36 × 16 m.
  - Three-aisled basilica with two narthexes and three semicircular apses; late 11th/early 12th c. on an early-Christian church; one study gives 30 × 20 × 13.1 m [S].
  - Low stone building with tile roofs; tall separate square stone bell tower with three tiers of paired arched openings; stone-paved forecourt, cypresses, cliffs directly behind [P].
  - Famous marble ambo in the nave [S].
- **Agios Vissarion (metropolitan church, patron saint)** — 39.7084, 21.6263; ~33 × 31 m.
  - Large modern neo-Byzantine cross-in-square; grey-beige ashlar with red brick arches around triple windows; broad tiled dome on a low drum; attached square bell tower with pyramidal tile roof; white arcaded porch with red arches [P].
- **Agios Konstantinos** — 39.7023, 21.6220 (SW part); ~29 × 23 m.
  - White-rendered modern Byzantine style; tiled central dome on a tall drum; tile-capped apses; bell tower; red window surrounds; small pine-topped hill behind (OSM has a Profitis Ilias chapel there) [P][OSM][?].
- **Town hall** — 39.7072, 21.6233; ~28 × 24 m.
  - 2-storey white corner building with chamfered arched entrance; blue window frames; blue iron balcony wrapping the upper floor; wide-eaved terracotta hipped roof; blue "ΔΗΜΑΡΧΕΙΟ" lettering; sheer rock behind [P 2014].
- **Railway station** — S edge.
  - 2-storey, 5-bay block; ochre-yellow render `#E0B24A`; grey stone quoins and string course; pale-blue doors and window frames; low hipped tile roof; single-storey wing; old stone goods shed with a round gable window [P].
  - Opened 1886 as the Thessaly Railways terminus, rebuilt for standard gauge 2001; 2 platforms, 3 tracks, footbridge; attributed to Evaristo de Chirico [S Wikipedia only].
- **Natural History Museum of Meteora & Mushroom Museum** — Pindou 20; 39.7017, 21.6307 (SE).
  - Modern 2-storey flat-roofed box in dark charcoal render, glass balcony balustrade, grey paver forecourt [P]. One tourism page calls it a neoclassical former school [?].
- **Stadium** — W end, 39.7008, 21.6126.
  - Pitch ~107 × 61 m, with the Vlachaveio indoor hall (~47 × 29 m) beside it [OSM]. Stands and track not verified [?].
- **Others locals would expect [OSM][S]:**
  - Digital Projection Centre of Meteora history (39.7087, 21.6191)
  - Hellenic Culture (schoolbook) Museum (39.7045, 21.6269)
  - Sinani park (S, ~180 × 80 m)
  - old wash-house and ancient inscription near Koimisi
  - churches of St John the Baptist and Panagia [S]
  - the abandoned, never-finished Kalampaka–Kozani rail earthworks heading NW

### Kastraki (pop. ~1,000; 270–280 m) [S]
- Amphitheatre-like village directly under Holy Spirit, Sourloti and Pixari; protected traditional settlement with a height limit [S].
- **Houses:** 1–2 storeys, stone or white-rendered, almost all red clay tile roofs (~95% [P estimate]), flowered yards. Some old mansions have a stone ground floor and timber upper floor [S].
- **Streets:** narrow, winding, cobbled in the old core, Mesochori [S][P].
- **Landmarks:**
  - central square with old school under Holy Spirit [S]
  - church of Sts Peter & Paul (~27 × 15 m) [OSM]
  - Agios Nikolaos (frescoes 1794) and Agios Athanasios [S]
  - Koimisi church at Adrachti [S]
  - Museum of Geological Formations [OSM]
  - campsites on the W approach
  - football pitch far to the NW [OSM]
  - traditional fountains and wells [S]

## 4. Landscape

- **Between the rocks [S][P]:** dense broadleaf deciduous forest (oaks, hop-hornbeam) filling every gully; oriental plane with willow and alder along streams; cypresses planted at monasteries and churches; pines on small town hills.
- **Open slopes behind Great Meteoron/Varlaam [P]:** dry grass with round dark evergreen shrubs (kermes/holm oak type; species not confirmed [?]).
- **Plain S and W of town [P]:** patchwork of small flat fields with scattered sheds; green in late spring, straw-gold by July.
- **Pineios [P][OSM]:** very wide braided pale gravel bed with thin water channels and a riparian tree strip, passing SW/S of town about 1–1.5 km from the centre.
- **Backdrop:**
  - Koziakas, S/SW across the river: steep, dark-forested lower slopes, grey limestone crags, bare summit ridge [P]. Height (~1,900 m) is from general knowledge [?].
  - Antichasia, N/NE: low rounded oak-covered hills [S].
- **Roads [OSM][P]:**
  - EO6/E92 Trikala–Ioannina runs through town as Trikalon → Ioanninon.
  - Monastery road "Kalampakas–Meteoron": 2-lane asphalt, Kalampaka → Kastraki → St Nicholas → Roussanou → junction. One branch goes to Varlaam and Great Meteoron (dead-end car park); the other to Holy Trinity and St Stephen. Low stone parapets, lay-bys with coaches, viewpoints (Psaropetra at 39.7193, 21.6371).
  - Whether it closes into a true loop back to town was not verified [?].
- **Railway [OSM]:** single track, standard gauge, non-electrified; arrives from the SE and terminates at the station.

## 6. Uncertain / could not verify

- **Great Meteoron:** step count (146 vs ~300 vs 195 in one Greek text); elevation (613/615 m vs 534 m); summit area (OSM ~1.8 ha vs "50 stremmata"); dome height 24 m rests on one travel source; exact number of domes.
- **St Nicholas Anapafsas:** en.wiki says inactive as of Dec 2025 — check locally. Step count is only a tourism "about 140" average.
- **Roussanou:** true summit footprint (my ~30 × 20 m is a guess); build date 1530 vs 1545; "octagonal" dome from one guidebook.
- **Holy Trinity:** one dome vs two; whether there is a cross at the rock tip; the cable car's exact anchor points.
- **St Stephen:** bridge length and material; elevation (510 vs 575 m); number of domes on St Charalambos.
- **Rock heights:** OSM and Wikipedia disagree widely (Holy Spirit 355 vs 600 m; Badovas 334 vs 447; Ambaria 308 vs 400; Pixari 408 vs 524). Use relative heights and photos. Adrachti's height not found.
- **Rock identities:** my naming of the cliffs behind the town hall and station is tentative. Sourloti, Modi, Alsos, Pixari and Ambaria silhouettes not individually checked.
- **Town squares:** no confirmation of a fountain; squares redesigned after my 2014 photos, so current paving, furniture and town hall colours may differ; Plateia Dimoula location unknown.
- **Roof share** (85–90% tiled in Kalampaka, ~95% in Kastraki) is my estimate from one aerial photo, not a count.
- **Museum building:** photo shows a modern dark box; one page says neoclassical former school.
- **Station:** de Chirico attribution and listed status are Wikipedia-only; how much of the building is original vs the 2001 rebuild is unknown.
- **Stadium:** only the pitch outline and adjacent hall confirmed; no photo of stands or track.
- **Agios Konstantinos:** identified from the Commons category name plus OSM position only. Build dates for it and Agios Vissarion not found.
- **Monastery road:** closed-loop topology not verified.
- **Vegetation:** kermes oak not confirmed by a species list. Koziakas height not sourced.
- **Colours:** every hex is a visual estimate from photos taken in mixed seasons and light.
